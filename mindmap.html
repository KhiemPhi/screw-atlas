SALSA Verb Map

Every verb of the SALSA task vocabulary, linked to the objects it applies to. Click a node to expand or fold it: SALSA → verb group → verb → dataset → object. Objects marked ▶ open their screw placement and the robot executing it. An object's colour says how far SALSA covers that (verb, object) pair. Links come from p3/vocab.py.

Disclaimer. Only links with a ▶ have been simulated (Kinova, ground-truth part, shared executor; MuJoCo rigid-body physics: nothing breaks, cuts, deforms, sticks or flows). Grey links are verbs that apply to the object but have no SALSA task yet. These 25 verbs have no simulated task for any object: go, return, stop, grasp, grab, hold, catch, scratch, clear, break, pierce, fork, wrap, unwrap, switch, unplug, chisel, bend, pluck, join, glue, weld, drill, assemble, disassemble. Brown "proposed" links are our guesses, not part of the vocabulary.
